01 — Overview

Most nations built apps on top of somebody else's infrastructure.

India built the infrastructure — and gave it away for free. Identity, payments, commerce, health, documents — every layer a public utility. Every citizen a user by birth.

Read this as documentation, not decoration. These endpoints are live. They serve a billion people, right now, while you finish this sentence.

02 — Endpoints
The public stack.

Eight primary endpoints in the India DPI. Each one an independent module, each one composable, each one deployed at national scale. Response times measured in milliseconds, adoption measured in billions.

POST /identity/verify

Aadhaar — identity layer

Verify identity for 1.42 billion residents. Biometric or one-time password. Sub-second response, zero cost per authentication. The largest identity system ever built.

uptime 99.98%
auths/day ~90M
shipped Sep 2009
POST /payments/send

UPI — payments rail

Move money between any two Indian bank accounts. 18.7 billion transactions a month. Settles in under a second. No fees on the rails. Now interoperable with France, UAE, Singapore, Sri Lanka.

latency < 1s
merchant fee 0%
shipped Apr 2016
GET /commerce/discover

ONDC — open commerce

Query products and services across every registered seller in India. One protocol, no platform monopoly. The kirana and the multinational speak the same API.

sellers 630K+
categories all
shipped Apr 2022
POST /documents/fetch

DigiLocker — document layer

Retrieve any government-issued document — Aadhaar, PAN, licence, degree, ration card. Legally equivalent to the physical original. 300 million citizens carry their government in their pocket.

docs stored 7B+
issuers 2,300+
shipped Jul 2015
POST /toll/deduct

FASTag — roads & mobility

Deduct highway tolls in motion. RFID-based, prepaid, no stop. 98% of national highway toll collection. The queue at the plaza is a solved problem.

coverage 98%
daily txns 10M+
shipped Dec 2019
POST /vaccination/schedule

CoWIN — health delivery

Deployed for 2.2 billion vaccine doses across 22 languages. Peak throughput: 8.6 million doses per day. Handed to Indonesia, Sri Lanka, Vietnam as open source.

doses served 2.2B
peak/day 8.6M
shipped Jan 2021
POST /tax/file

GSTN — tax network

One indirect-tax API for 14 million registered businesses. Real-time invoicing, reconciliation, filing. Killed the tax inspector's clipboard.

businesses 14M
invoices/day 25M+
shipped Jul 2017
GET /health/records

ABHA — portable health ID

A health identity that travels with the patient, not the hospital. Records portable across every provider in the country. Consent-based access, always.

ids issued 700M+
linked records 50Cr+
shipped Sep 2021
